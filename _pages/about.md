I'm a **Computer Science M.S. student** at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC) and a **Graduate Research Assistant** in the [AI-Theory Oriented Molecular Science (ATOMS) Lab](#current-research). My work sits where formal methods meet practice: proving software correct in Lean 4, and finding out where real systems break.

[Projects](/projects/){: .btn} [Teaching](/teaching/){: .btn .btn--inverse} [Full CV](/cv/){: .btn .btn--inverse}

## Current research

I'm funded by an **Amazon Scientist grant** to work on **probabilistic programming in Lean 4**, with applications to molecular simulation. In the ATOMS Lab I:

- formalize statistical thermodynamic ensembles and the mathematics needed to derive their properties;
- implement and verify Markov chain Monte Carlo code, both in general and for simulations of molecular systems;
- build training materials that teach scientists and engineers to use these methods and to reason rigorously about chemistry and chemical engineering.
